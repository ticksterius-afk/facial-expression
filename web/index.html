Mien

Reads facial expressions in real time and names them — the seven primary emotions, the fifteen compound emotions, and social, cognitive and physical states — and shows which muscles of the face are doing the talking.

Everything runs on this device. No images or data leave it.

Faces show expressions, not guaranteed feelings — the same movement can mean different things in different contexts and cultures. Treat the labels as descriptions of what the face is doing.

Calibrate your neutral face

Everyone's resting face is different. Look at the screen with a relaxed, neutral expression for three seconds so expressions are measured from your baseline.

Settings

Mien uses MediaPipe Face Landmarker for tracking and a FACS-based model (Ekman & Friesen; Du, Tao & Martinez; Keltner; Tracy & Robins; Prkachin & Solomon) to name expressions. See the repository's docs/RESEARCH.md for the science and its limits.
